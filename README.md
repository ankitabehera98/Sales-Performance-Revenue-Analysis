# Sales Performance & Revenue Analysis
### B2B Sales Analytics | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing B2B sales performance, tracking revenue generation and evaluating sales team productivity using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to build a structured sales reporting system to monitor revenue trends, compare sales performance against targets, evaluate individual sales representatives and present key business insights through a KPI dashboard.

The project uses simulated B2B sales data for practical Sales Operations and analytics experience.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends.
- Evaluate individual sales representative performance.
- Track won, lost and open deals.
- Compare actual revenue against sales targets.
- Calculate important sales KPIs.
- Develop a consolidated sales performance dashboard.

## 🔍 Project Methodology

**1. Sales Data Management**
- Organized simulated B2B sales transaction records.
- Maintained deal values, sales representatives and deal statuses.
- Structured sales information for reporting and analysis.

**2. Revenue Analysis**
- Calculated total revenue generated.
- Evaluated average deal value.
- Compared actual revenue against assigned sales targets.

**3. Sales Representative Performance**
- Analyzed individual sales contributions.
- Compared revenue generated by different representatives.
- Identified top-performing sales representatives.

**4. Monthly Sales Analysis**
- Analyzed monthly revenue trends.
- Compared monthly performance against targets.
- Evaluated changes in sales performance over time.

**5. KPI Dashboard Development**
- Consolidated important sales performance indicators.
- Presented revenue and deal performance metrics.
- Created a centralized view for sales performance monitoring.
